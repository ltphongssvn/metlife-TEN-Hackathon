The Immigration Timeline Reality
Understanding visa and residency process:

F-1 Student Visa (during studies):
- Valid while enrolled full-time
- Limited work authorization (on-campus, CPT, OPT)
- Must maintain valid status

Optional Practical Training (OPT):
- 12 months standard (after degree completion)
- 24-month STEM extension (science, technology, engineering, math)
- Provides work authorization to gain U.S. experience

H-1B Work Visa (employment-based):
- Requires employer sponsorship
- Initial 3-year period, renewable for 3 more
- Allows dual intent (can pursue green card)
- Competitive lottery system for new applications

Green Card (Permanent Residence):
- Employment-based most common for students
- EB-2 or EB-3 categories
- Employer sponsors process
- Can take 2-10+ years depending on country of origin
- Once obtained, can work anywhere, own business

Citizenship (after 5 years with green card):
- Full rights including voting
- U.S. passport
- Can never be deported
- Can sponsor family members

Plan education and career with these timelines in mind.
